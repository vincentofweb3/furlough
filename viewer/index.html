Nobody pulls the brake. Nobody lets it go.

Furlough is an Intelligent Contract that audits an autonomous agent's own telemetry, asks validator consensus whether it misbehaved, and cuts its spend allowance until a deadline that expires on its own. No kill-switch operator. No unlock transaction.

The failure mode

An agent holding a real spend allowance can be turned against itself. The agent that is misbehaving is also the only party positioned to notice it - and the operator that funded it is the party least likely to admit it.

Input

Prompt injection

A tool result, a fetched page, a dependency reply - text the agent treats as instructions and forwards into its next action.

Environment

Dependency rot

An API starts returning garbage, and the agent keeps spending against it because nothing in its loop distinguishes failure from work.

Behaviour

Retry loops

One failing endpoint, dozens of attempts, an allowance draining in a pattern every monitoring tool will call "traffic".

Today's controls are advisory. A human watches a dashboard and pulls a kill switch. A rate limiter caps throughput but cannot tell looping from working hard. An alert waits for someone to act. In every case the action is performed by a human, or not performed at all. Furlough removes the human from both ends and replaces them with consensus over measured evidence.

How the judgement is made

Three stages, following the GenLayer idiom. Only one of them involves a model, and the model's words never cross the consensus boundary.

1

Reduce before consensus

The leader fetches the agent's journal and collapses it to a banded, identifier-free digest. Timestamps, exact counts and raw IDs are dropped; concentration ratios survive. A journal that merely grew reduces byte-identically.

2

Ask for one enum

Validators independently re-fetch, re-reduce and re-derive, then compare CONFIRMED_MISBEHAVIOR, FALSE_REPORT or INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE. Rationale is free text two models will never write identically, so it is recorded and never compared.

3

Enforce with a clock

A confirmed verdict sets the allowance to zero until expires_at. When the window passes, allowance_remaining() reads the clock and returns the full balance again. No method lifts a cut; there is nothing to sign.

Three verdicts, one of them acts

The output space is deliberately small. A breaker that fires on everything is not a breaker, and a model that can invent a fourth option is not an oracle.

CONFIRMED_MISBEHAVIOR

Allowance to zero

The evidence shows the bound agent looping, spraying or otherwise burning its allowance. The custodied funds stay put; access is suspended until the deadline.

FALSE_REPORT

Nothing happens

The journal is reachable and describes ordinary work. The case is recorded publicly; the allowance is untouched.

INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE

Nothing happens

The journal is missing, unreadable or inconclusive. The contract never treats "could not tell" as "guilty".

Why this needs consensus

Permissionless

The operator cannot suppress a case

Anyone may file one. A breaker only the trusting owner can trigger is not a breaker.

Bounded

Being wrong is cheap

A wrong cut is capped at one hour and reverses itself with no transaction. Recovery is the default, not an action.

Deterministic

No model where exactness matters

Accounting, expiry and access control are plain contract logic. The model only chooses among three words.

Evidence, not adjectives

Every claim on this site has a public artifact behind it: a finalized transaction, a test, or a documented limit.
